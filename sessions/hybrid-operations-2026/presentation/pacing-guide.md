## How to use the checkpoints

Check the clock when leaving a section, not after every slide. If the room needs more explanation, spend the time and adjust the next checkpoint. Protect the meaning and debrief of each required demo. Questions remains the Q&A beat; Thank you is the closing handoff.

The added platform section suggests two minutes for guest/platform distinction, three for SCVMM architecture, one for the prepared workloads, and two for VMware. The later SCVMM workload demonstration suggests five minutes. These replace the earlier TBD entries as planning estimates.

For asynchronous onboarding, policy evaluation, Sentinel analytics or Flux reconciliation, show the submitted state and move to genuine dated prepared evidence when available. Do not spend an unlimited portion of the session polling or imply that a proposed duration guarantees completion.

If working to a shorter event slot, use the checkpoints to decide where to be concise and take detailed discussion afterward. Do not silently remove the required AWS management outcomes, monitoring workspace explanation or security demonstrations merely to meet arithmetic. Presenter acceptance and live evidence remain separate.
